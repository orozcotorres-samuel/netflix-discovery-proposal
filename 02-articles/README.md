## Suggested sources to cover

Delete as you add them. These are the categories a Netflix PM would expect you to have read:

**Netflix's own writing**
- [ ] Netflix Technology Blog — recommender system architecture, artwork personalization, row ranking
- [ ] Netflix Research — the recommendations and search publication pages
- [ ] Netflix quarterly shareholder letters — any language on engagement, "hours viewed," or discovery
- [ ] Tudum — how Netflix positions its own editorial discovery layer

**The problem space**
- [ ] Published research on choice overload / the paradox of choice in streaming
- [ ] Industry survey data on time-to-selection and abandonment before playback
- [ ] Coverage of subscriber churn drivers in streaming

**The competitive layer**
- [ ] JustWatch / Reelgood — how the cross-service aggregators describe their own value proposition
- [ ] Coverage of Amazon's IMDb strategy and its role in discovery
- [ ] Any competitor shipping conversational or natural-language discovery

**Feasibility**
- [ ] Netflix's public work on LLMs, semantic search, or natural-language interfaces
- [ ] Precedents for conversational discovery in other consumer products
